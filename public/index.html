    Deploy to your own Vercel account

    Publishing here is great for showing someone what you built. Deploying to Vercel makes it yours — your account, your domain, your bill — and it keeps working whether or not this builder does.

    How to get a token

    1. In Vercel, open Settings → Tokens and click Create Token.
    2. Under Scope, pick the single project you want to deploy to — not your whole account.
    3. Copy the token and paste it below.

    A project-scoped token is the important part. It can only touch that one project, so VibeSafe can never see your other projects or their environment variables.

    Your token is encrypted before it is stored and is never sent back to your browser. VibeSafe uses it only to deploy this project — nothing else.
