About VibeSafe Builder

VibeSafe Builder (Vibe Safe Builder) is the AI app builder at vibesafebuilder.com, operated by SG Digital Ventures LLC. Our security scanner, VibeSafe, is only at vibesafe.info. We are not affiliated with vibesafe.net. Describe a web app in plain English to build it with cost estimates, version history, and security checks. Our sibling product, vibesafe.info, focuses on scanning code for security issues.

01Why this exists

We researched thousands of reviews of the major AI app builders. The same handful of complaints kept surfacing: users get billed for the AI's own failed generations, costs arrive as a surprise, one requested change quietly breaks ten other things with no way back, the AI repeats a fix that already failed, and finished apps ship with exposed databases or hardcoded keys. Each of those became a specific, testable feature in VibeSafe Builder rather than a marketing claim — see how it works.
