Report a published app

Apps published here are built by our customers, not by us. If one of them breaks the rules, tell us and we'll review it. You don't need an account, and you can report anonymously.

If someone is in danger
Contact your local emergency services first. For child sexual abuse material, report to the IWF or NCMEC CyberTipline as well as here — we act on these immediately, but those organisations can act far beyond one website.

What happens next

Reports go to a person, not a queue that nobody reads. Apps that clearly break the rules are taken offline, and the link stops working for everyone. Where a report is borderline we look at the app before doing anything, because taking down someone's working project by mistake is its own harm.

We don't share your email with the app's owner. If you gave one, we may write to ask what you saw.

You can also email contact@vibesafebuilder.com directly, which is the right route for legal notices and anything that needs a paper trail.
