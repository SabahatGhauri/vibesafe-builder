Pricing questions

What happens if I go over the $10/month Managed budget?

Generation pauses until your next billing cycle — you're never silently charged more. Switch to your own API key in Settings to keep going immediately.

How much does a typical build actually cost on my own key?

Most single-file app generations run $0.05–$0.20 depending on complexity, billed by Anthropic at their standard per-token rates. We add no markup on top.

Can I switch between Bring Your Own Key and Managed?

Yes, any time in Settings — add a key or sign in to Managed, nothing about your project or version history changes.

Do failed generations ever cost me anything?

You're never charged extra — the $15/month Managed fee is flat, and BYOK failures never count toward your session's build-spend meter or cap. One nuance on Managed: a failed attempt still draws from your $10/mo included usage, since that's real Anthropic cost we absorb either way — it just never counts against your visible “builds this month.”
